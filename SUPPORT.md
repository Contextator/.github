what goes wrong on a first install.

Searching [the existing issues](https://github.com/Contextator/Contextator/issues?q=is%3Aissue) — closed
ones included — is usually faster than waiting for an answer.

## Asking

Open an [issue](https://github.com/Contextator/Contextator/issues/new/choose). A question is a perfectly
good issue; it does not have to be a bug report. What makes it answerable in one round:

- what you expected and what happened instead,
- the version or commit, and how it is deployed (the single container, `npm run dev`, something else),
- the log lines around the failure — checked for tokens before you paste them.

## What this is not

- **Not a security channel.** A vulnerability goes through [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), never an issue.
- **Not a guaranteed response time.** Contextator is maintained by one person. Questions are answered when
  they are read, and nothing here is a support contract.
